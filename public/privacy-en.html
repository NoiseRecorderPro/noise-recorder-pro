Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Noise Recorder works on your device without an account. We do not track you for advertising or use third-party usage analytics.

Data and permissions

Microphone access is used to measure sound levels and record audio. Camera access is optional for video recording. Photos permission is only used to add recorded videos, not read your existing library.

Recordings, sound level readings, dates, durations, notes, and settings are stored on your device for playback, saved records, charts, and reports. We do not receive this content unless you choose to share it with us.

Sharing and services

Files are shared only with destinations you select. Apple handles purchases; we do not receive your full payment details. Depending on your settings, Apple may back up App data or sync videos saved to Photos. These services follow their own privacy policies.

This website uses GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support

If you email us, we receive your address and message. Feedback drafts include App/iOS versions, device model, membership status, language, and region; you can edit these before sending. We use this information only for support and retain it as needed to resolve your request or meet legal requirements.

Your choices and deletion

Saved records remain until you delete them in the App or delete the App. Exported files and backups must be managed separately. You can revoke microphone, camera, and Photos permissions in iOS Settings, and contact us to request deletion of support emails, except records legally required to be retained.

Contact and updates

Questions or deletion requests: noise-recorder-pro@qq.com. If this policy changes, we will update the effective date.
